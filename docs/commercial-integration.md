# Commercial integration

ChainBloom's protocol has no token, sale mechanism, royalty, protocol fee,
reward, or price oracle. A business may still charge for its own custody,
wallet, hosting, rendering, moderation, support, or creative services. Those
relationships are off-protocol and must not be described as protocol-enforced.

Before launch, define who controls carrier keys, who can approve a PSBT, who
pays miner fees, what happens after key loss or invalid spend, how reorgs are
shown, and which service promises survive world expiry. Separate confirmed and
provisional UI. Disclose software status, miner-fee volatility, public graph
linkage, irreversible spends, and lack of an administrator.

Custody, money transmission, consumer protection, sanctions, privacy,
advertising, intellectual property, contest/gambling, securities, tax, and
record-retention obligations vary by product and jurisdiction. Obtain qualified
counsel; this repository does not classify an integration.

Do not use the ChainBloom name to imply endorsement. Avoid claims of guaranteed
continuity, scarcity value, investment return, permanent hosting, or identical
rendering. Establish support boundaries and incident response before accepting
customer funds or keys. Pin and audit package versions and validate extracted
transactions independently before broadcast.
